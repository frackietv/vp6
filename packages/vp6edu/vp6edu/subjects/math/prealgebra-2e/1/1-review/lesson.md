## 1.1 Introduction to Whole Numbers

Place value, names of numbers and rounding: [1.1 Introduction to Whole Numbers](lesson:math/prealgebra-2e/1.1).

**1.** What is the digit $6$ in $4{,}629{,}015$ worth?

```answer
Number(600000)
```

**2.** Write *two million, eighty thousand, five* with digits.

```answer
Number(2080005)
```

**3.** Round $48{,}652$ to the nearest thousand.

```answer
Number(49000)
```

## Mixed Problems

Some problems take two steps. Decide what to find first, find it, then use it.

**Example.** Sam buys 3 shirts at \$18 each and pays with a \$100 bill. How much change does Sam get?

*Solution.* The shirts cost $3 \times 18 = 54$ dollars; the change is $100 - 54 = 46$ dollars.

**12.** A class of $28$ students collects $13$ cans each, then packs the cans into boxes of $20$. How many boxes can be filled?

```answer
Number(18)
```

**13.** Before working out $4{,}812 + 2{,}391$ exactly, estimate it by rounding each number to the nearest thousand. What is the estimate?

```answer
Number(7000)
```

## Lab

The lab is an arithmetic game: one minute of questions from all four operations, with your best score kept from game to game. **Run the lab** (F5), press **Start** and answer as many as you can; press Enter after each answer.

Then change the program:

1. Find `new_question` in `Form1`. Make the questions harder as your score goes up (bigger numbers after every five right).
2. Add rounding questions: "Round 4,862 to the nearest hundred".
3. The best score is kept with `SaveSetting`. Keep a best score for each operation.
