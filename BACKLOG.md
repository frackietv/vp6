# VP6 backlog

- **[Education]** Step 1, the curriculum package: `packages/vp6edu/` (vp6edu, its own version and wheel, VP6 depending on it as on vp6help; its core without PySide6), holding subjects as data, nothing in it specific to mathematics (Physics and Computer Science come later): a subject has learning paths; a path is a graph of nodes, each node a book (Mathematics: Prealgebra 2e → Elementary Algebra 2e → Intermediate Algebra 2e → Algebra and Trigonometry 2e → Precalculus 2e → Calculus Volume 1 → Volume 2 → Volume 3, with Introductory Statistics 2e branching from Intermediate Algebra 2e); a node has chapters, a chapter has lessons (one per OpenStax section: its number, title and openstax.org link); a lesson is a folder `vp6edu/subjects/<subject>/<book>/<chapter>/<section>/` holding `lesson.md`, `exercises.py` and `lab/`. The whole outline of the nine books goes in now, from their tables of contents, with every lesson marked planned until it's written. Also: `load_subjects()`; lesson ids (`math/prealgebra-2e/1.2`); each lesson's prerequisites (the earlier sections on its path, and links of its own); lessons that books share referenced rather than copied (Precalculus 2e's chapters 1–11 mostly follow Algebra and Trigonometry 2e's 3–13, Calculus Volume 2's chapters 1–2 are Volume 1's 5–6, Volume 3's chapter 1 is Volume 2's 7: check section by section). The licence: OpenStax's books are CC BY 4.0, so every lesson carries an attribution line (book, authors, section, link, "adapted from"), and the text is our own adaptation, never pasted pages. A validator (`vp6edu-check`), tests, and a section in `docs/source-reference.md`.
- **[Education]** Step 2, mathematics in Markdown: inline `$…$` and display `$$…$$` formulas in a TeX subset: fractions, exponents and subscripts, roots, Greek letters, relations and operators (≤ ≠ ± · ×), absolute values, parentheses that grow, sums, integrals and limits with their bounds, matrices, `cases`, `\text`, an overline for repeating decimals, vectors. A Qt-free module in vp6help parses them and lays them out (boxes with baselines, TeX's spacing classes). vp6help's viewer and the MarkdownBox (Preview and Visual) draw them in the text's font and colour (light and dark, the font size followed, high DPI). The source stays `$…$` in `Text` and is copied as TeX. A formula that doesn't parse shows its source in red. Documented with the Markdown docs, shown on the Kitchen Sink's MarkdownBox page, and tested by comparing layouts and grabs.
- **[Education]** Step 3, checking answers: `vp6edu.answers`, pure Python (no sympy). A student's typed maths is read leniently (`2x`, `x^2`, `3(x+1)`, `sqrt(x)`, `√`, `π`, `½`, mixed numbers `2 1/3`, percents, `1,000`) and checked by kind:
  - a choice (one or many);
  - a number: exact with `Fraction`, or within a tolerance, in a required form when asked (simplest form, a decimal to n places, scientific notation);
  - an expression: equivalence by evaluating at random points (exact fractions where possible), and a required form when asked (factored, expanded, simplified radicals);
  - an equation's solutions as a set, an inequality's or interval's solution (`x > 3`, `(3, ∞)`, a number line), an ordered pair or tuple, a point or line placed on a Graph (step 4), units.

  Known mistakes (a sign error, the reciprocal, an unsimplified answer) get their own feedback. Every check returns right, not yet, or unreadable, with a message. An `Exercise` holds its prompt (Markdown with maths), its answer, hints given one at a time, and a worked solution. Functions in a lesson's `exercises.py` make exercises from a seeded `random.Random`, so each try can have new numbers. Tests cover every kind.
- **[Education]** Step 4, a Graph control for mathematics (`vp6/graph.py`, Toolbox category Graphics):
  - a coordinate plane: axes, a grid, labels, ticks in multiples of π for trigonometry, equal or free scales, pan and zoom with the mouse;
  - plots: functions given as Python callables or as expression text (`AddFunction`, with a domain; holes and asymptotes left unjoined), parametric and polar curves, points, segments, rays, vectors, polygons, regions shaded between curves, half-planes of inequalities, labels with maths (step 2);
  - a number-line mode (open or closed points, intervals) for Prealgebra and inequalities;
  - a data mode for statistics: histograms, dot plots, box plots, scatter plots with a fitted line;
  - interaction: draggable points (`PointMoved`), `Click(X, Y)` in graph coordinates, a point or line placed as an answer (read by step 3);
  - the graphics methods over it, `Picture`/`EditCopy`, light and dark.

  A Kitchen Sink page, the docs' generated section, and tests with grabs.
- **[Education]** Step 5, lesson projects and the Lesson window. A lesson opened from the Education tab (step 6) becomes a VP6 project made from the lesson's `lab/`, as `samples.create` makes a sample. Its .vp6p has a `lesson` key (the lesson's id) and the lesson's title as its Description. When the project already exists it's reopened, not made again. Opening such a project opens the Lesson window (an MDI child, `vp6/ide/lessonwindow.py`, its Qt-free part in `vp6/ide/logic/education.py`), with three tabs:
  - **Lesson:** the lesson's Markdown with maths; objectives, worked examples with Try It answers checked in place, key terms, the attribution footer and a link to the OpenStax section;
  - **Exercises:** one exercise after another, answered in a field or on a Graph, with Check, Hint, Show Solution, New Numbers and a score;
  - **Lab:** what to try with the lab project; F5 runs it; its forms are listed. The lab is plain VP6 code the student changes.

  Previous and Next go along the path. Progress is kept per lesson in the IDE's settings (read; exercises right and tried; lab run), with Reset Progress. Tests use a stand-in lesson in a temporary subject.
- **[Education]** Step 6, the New Project dialog's fourth tab, Education, after New, Existing and Recent:
  - a Subject drop-down listing the subjects vp6edu has (Mathematics for now);
  - the subject's learning path drawn as a graph (a widget of its own): each book a node with its title and a progress bar, arrows between the books, the Statistics branch leaving Intermediate Algebra; laid out from the path's graph, scrolling when wider than the dialog, light and dark;
  - choosing a node lists its chapters and lessons in a tree, each lesson showing its state (planned ones greyed out; not started; in progress; done), with the lesson's description and prerequisites under the tree.

  Open makes or reopens the lesson project under the Location (by default a `VP6 Lessons` folder there, then subject/book/section) and opens it (`result_action` "lesson"). The tab, subject and node are remembered, and Help > Education opens the tab. Tests and screenshots (light, dark, macOS).
